The Problem With Ordering Agricultural Parts

Standard replacement parts for farm equipment have always had a few frustrating realities:

  • Discontinued parts for older tractors simply aren't available from dealers anymore
  • Minimum order quantities force you to buy 10 when you need 1
  • Long lead times from distributors mean days or weeks of downtime
  • High prices for specialty components that should cost a fraction of what dealers charge

For a small to mid-size farm operation in Hardin County, any of these can hit the bottom line hard.

Materials That Hold Up in the Field

Not every plastic is built for outdoor farm use. We stock materials matched to agricultural environments:

  • ASA — UV and weather resistant, holds up in direct sunlight without fading or cracking
  • PETG — tough and chemical-resistant, good for fuel or fluid contact areas
  • Nylon — high-strength, flexible under stress — great for load-bearing parts
  • ABS — durable and impact resistant for general-purpose applications

We'll recommend the right material for your specific use case before we quote the job.
