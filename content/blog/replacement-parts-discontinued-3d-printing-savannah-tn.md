## How We Recreate a Broken Part

You don't need to have the original CAD file or technical drawing. Here's what we work from:

**1. The broken piece itself**
If you have the broken part, that's usually enough. We measure it, model it in 3D software, and print it. Cracks, chips, and missing sections aren't a problem — we work from what's intact.

**2. A photo with measurements**
Text us a few photos with the key dimensions marked (or just the dimensions in a message). For simple parts, that's enough to quote and build.

**3. A reference part from something similar**
Sometimes a part that fits one brand fits another with a small modification. If you know of something close, let us know.

**4. Your own 3D file**
Have a file from an online parts database or CAD software? We'll print it directly.

## Matching the Right Material to the Job

Replacement parts need to perform like the original — or better. We choose material based on what the part does:

| Use Case | Recommended Material |
|---|---|
| General indoor parts | PLA or ABS |
| Outdoor / UV exposure | ASA |
| Flexible / snap-fit | TPU |
| Chemical exposure | PETG |
| High strength / load-bearing | Nylon |

If you're not sure, just describe what the part does and where it lives — we'll make the call.
