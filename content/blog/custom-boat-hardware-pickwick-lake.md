## UV-Resistant Materials for the Water

Standard plastics break down fast in direct sun, saltwater spray, and constant UV exposure. We use **ASA** for most outdoor marine applications — the same weather-resistant material used in automotive exterior parts. It holds color and structure through Tennessee summers on the water.

For parts that flex or need to grip, we use **TPU** (flexible polyurethane). For high-strength structural parts, we have **PETG** and **Nylon** on hand.
